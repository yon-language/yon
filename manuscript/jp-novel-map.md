# Jurassic Park — novel map (source material for the structure)

> Extracted from the supplied epub (Crichton, 1990 / 2012 Random House ebook),
> read in full in spine order. This is reference material for `01-jp-structure.md`.
> The verbatim Malcolm epigraphs below anchor the 7-part structure of the book.

## The engine of the novel (the thematic hinge)

A **problem of identity and counting**: InGen's control system verifies the
population against an *expected* number (the base count, **238**), so it is
structurally blind to **excess**. The animals breed (amphibian/frog DNA →
spontaneous female→male sex change in single-sex environments), the count stays
"correct," and the gap between the recorded number and the real number is where the
catastrophe lives. The system can detect a *shortfall*, never a *surplus*, of
identities. The book's fourth wall: this is an ontological error (identity defined by
a label/expected-tally instead of by what is observed) — exactly the class Yon makes
a type error. The opening crisis (an unreadable count) and the closing scene
(counting eggshell impressions in the raptor nest to discover a thriving colony)
are the same act of counting, bookending the collapse.

## The seven Iterations → verbatim Malcolm epigraphs

In this edition the Iteration title-page caption and the Malcolm epigraph are one
and the same line (`p01`–`p07` spine files). Verbatim:

1. **First Iteration** — *"At the earliest drawings of the fractal curve, few clues to the underlying mathematical structure will be seen."*
   Mainland: a "lizard" bites infants; a fragment reaches Columbia; the genetic-engineering marker is dismissed as contamination; a child's drawing says *dinosaur*. The data is all present but unreadable.
2. **Second Iteration** — *"With subsequent drawings of the fractal curve, sudden changes may appear."*
   Grant/Sattler summoned by Hammond; Biosyn recruits Nedry to steal embryos; the party reaches Isla Nublar and sees living dinosaurs. The sudden change: clones are real.
3. **Third Iteration** — *"Details emerge more clearly as the fractal curve is re-drawn."*
   The tour. Wu explains DNA-from-amber, the lysine contingency, "all female / can't breed," version numbers. The control room. Grant finds eggshell; Malcolm forces a recount → total climbs past 238 → **they're breeding.** Frog DNA deduced. The structural hinge.
4. **Fourth Iteration** — *"Inevitably, underlying instabilities begin to appear."*
   Nedry's sabotage + the storm kill security/fences; the T-rex attacks the cars; Nedry dies; *rana* (frog) DNA confirmed in every breeding species; wild maiasaur nests found.
5. **Fifth Iteration** — *"Flaws in the system will now become severe."*
   River/aviary traverse; T-rex hunt; Grant finds a juvenile **male** velociraptor bred in the wild — the all-female control has failed.
6. **Sixth Iteration** — *"System recovery may prove impossible."*
   Power restoration backfires; raptors besiege the lodge over the dying Malcolm; recovery keeps failing.
7. **Seventh Iteration** — *"Increasingly, the mathematics will demand the courage to face its implications."*
   Malcolm's deathbed lectures; Grant counts eggshell impressions in the raptor nest (34 born; a self-sustaining colony); the raptors line up to **migrate**; Hammond and Malcolm die; the island is burned.

## Frame material

- **Introduction — "The InGen Incident"** (essay, no Malcolm epigraph): faux-nonfiction on commercialized genetic engineering, unwatched because every scientist has a stake; the 1976 Genentech origin; the secret InGen crisis and quiet Chapter 11 (Oct 5, 1989). Front-matter epigraphs: *"You cannot recall a new form of life." — Erwin Chargaff, 1972* (paired with a Linnaeus 1797 line).
- **Prologue — "The Bite of the Raptor"**: a mauled worker; Dr. Carter sees defense/bite wounds, not a "backhoe accident"; the dying boy whispers *"raptor."* Establishes the cover-up before any dinosaur is named.
- **Epilogue — "San José"**: survivors detained; unknown animals eating lysine-rich crops on the mainland, moving daily "in a straight line — almost as straight as an arrow." Containment failed at the largest scale. Close: *"None of us is going anywhere, Dr. Grant."*

## Key scenes by hook (for chapter assignment)

**Counting (the population-tally engine):**
- III/c20 — Wu: "our animals are counted by computer every few minutes… if one were missing we'd know at once." Population = 238.
- III/c22 — Arnold's tracking map + 15-min category tally; sensors cover only **92%** (not the jungle river).
- III/c28 — **the count is wrong**: Malcolm makes Arnold search for *more* than the base; total climbs 239→283. *"You only tracked the expected number… The problem was, you had more than the expected number."* → "They're breeding."
- VII/c55–57 — **the nest count**: 34 born; 33 infants + 22 juveniles alive — a colony far outside the recorded population.

**Breeding / "they're breeding":**
- lysine contingency (III/c20); eggshell found (III/c27); seven breeding sites (III/c28–29); wild maiasaur nests (IV/c43); juvenile male raptor (V/c46); cause = *rana* frog DNA → spontaneous sex change, confirmed c34/c53.

**Genome / gap-filling:**
- III/c19 — paleo-DNA from amber; computers **fill gaps**; only a few % analyzed; "error in line 1201."
- III/c21 — **"Version 4.4"**: dropouts/patches/gaps filled with best guesses; "like old photographs that had been retouched"; software version numbers; *"There isn't any reality here."* (Wu).
- III/c22 — Arnold: "It is like software. As we discover the glitches… a new version."

**Malcolm's chaos lectures:** II/c14 (nonlinearity, sensitive dependence, butterfly effect, "an accident waiting to happen"); III/c18 ("such isolation is impossible… cannot be done"); III/c29 (self-similarity; "life is a series of encounters… wholly unpredictable"); IV/c41 (the "Malcolm Effect" phase-space collapse, "we disagreed with it, and ignored it"); VII/c52 ("The planet is not in jeopardy. We are in jeopardy.").

**Human/operational failures (NOT structural-data — mark as framing, not Yon-catchable):**
Nedry's sabotage (the *trigger*); the storm; Hammond's cost-cutting/understaffing (~20 people, "automate wherever we could"); management ignoring Muldoon/Wu/Malcolm. These are the *occasions* of collapse; the *cause* in Crichton's design is the structural counting blindness. Yon closes the structural gap, not these.
